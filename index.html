About Us

Remember My Name Studios develops original formats for television, radio and podcasts. Our ideas span entertainment competitions, factual series, and programmes built around music and personal stories.

Our ideas often start with something we’ve noticed in everyday life: a game people play at weekends, a department store lift, or a skill someone wants to pass on. We build programmes around what people can do, what they know and what happens when they get a chance to show it.
